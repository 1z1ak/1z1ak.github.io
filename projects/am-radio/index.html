AM Radio Receiver

Designed and assembled a simple AM radio receiver by integrating a tuned LC circuit, diode detector, and operational amplifier to receive and amplify radio signals.

Course ENSC 220
Project Type Circuits Laboratory
Focus Analog Electronics

Project Overview

The objective of this project was to build and test a basic AM radio receiver. The circuit was divided into three main stages: a tuner to select radio frequencies, a detector to extract the audio signal, and an amplifier to increase the signal strength for headphone output.

The project involved assembling electronic components on a breadboard, constructing a custom inductor, and testing the circuit to understand how different components work together in an analog communication system.

Circuit Design

The receiver consists of three functional stages, each responsible for processing a different part of the incoming radio signal.

01 / TUNING

LC Tuner

Built an LC resonant circuit using a custom-made inductor and capacitors. The tuner selects a desired AM radio frequency through resonance, allowing the receiver to focus on a particular station.

02 / DETECTION

AM Detector

Used a diode-based detector circuit to rectify the received AM signal. Capacitors and resistors smooth the rectified waveform to recover the original audio information.

03 / AMPLIFICATION

Audio Amplifier

Integrated a TL072 operational amplifier to increase the recovered audio signal. The amplification stage provides sufficient signal strength to drive the connected headphones.

The receiver was assembled using a combination of passive components and active electronic devices.
