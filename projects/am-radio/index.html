ELECTRONICS · CIRCUIT DESIGN

AM Radio

A hands-on course project focused on building a simple AM radio circuit. The project involved making an inductor from scratch, assembling the circuit on a PCB and connecting the completed circuit to a radio.

PROJECT TYPE Course Project
MY WORK Inductor & Circuit Assembly
FOCUS Electronics & Prototyping

01. Project Overview

The objective of this project was to build a simple AM radio using an electronic circuit and a manually constructed inductor.

Rather than using a fully assembled radio module, the project involved working directly with the individual components. I built the inductor myself and used it together with the circuit on the PCB before connecting the setup to the radio.

02. Building the Inductor

One of the main hands-on parts of the project was constructing the inductor ourselves. This involved making the coil rather than relying on a pre-made component.

Building the inductor gave me practical experience working with electronic components and helped me understand how a physical component becomes part of a larger circuit.

03. PCB Circuit Assembly

After constructing the inductor, I used it together with the electronic circuit on the PCB. This stage involved working with the circuit components and connecting the inductor as part of the overall setup.

The project allowed me to work directly with a PCB and see how individual electronic components are brought together to form a complete circuit.

04. Final Connection & Demonstration

Once the inductor and PCB circuit were assembled, the setup was connected to the radio. This brought together the individually constructed component and the circuit into the final project.

05. What I Learned

This project gave me practical experience with basic electronics and circuit assembly. Building the inductor myself helped me become more familiar with individual electronic components, while assembling the PCB circuit gave me experience connecting components into a functioning setup.

It also gave me an opportunity to connect classroom concepts with a physical electronics project and understand the process of building a circuit from individual parts.
